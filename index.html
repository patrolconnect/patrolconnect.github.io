Paperwork

One guided entry. Your agency's form. Your review.

The PC Statement Generator and Search Warrant Generator produce a populated Word document on the agency or county template. Booking and Tow Sheet Generator produce the PDF. Every generator asks the officer to review the output before filing.

  • Charges from Missouri's charge code database
  • A 17-character VIN fills year, make and model
  • Email the finished document to others, with your own copy

In the field

References that answer at the roadside.

Missouri statutes and charge codes, case law, the 2024 Emergency Response Guidebook, VIN decoding and IP lookup sit beside the document modules on one Home page.

  • DWI timers keep time when the phone locks
  • ERG isolation distances exactly as printed
  • VIN results read aloud, with a mute toggle

Every Module

Grouped as officers see them on the Home page. Open Details for data handling and limits. Agency administrators can turn any module off.

Casework

Documents from one guided entry

  • PC Statement Generator

    A Missouri probable cause statement on the agency template, with optional AI review of the narrative.

    Output: Word document

    Details for PC Statement Generator

    Data: The AI review sends the charge list and narrative to OpenAI without redaction; the help text says so.

    Limits: Missouri PC statements only. Not legal advice.

  • Search Warrant Generator

    A Missouri search warrant application on a county template, with a reusable snippet library.

    Output: Word document

    Details for Search Warrant Generator

    Data: Draft contents reach Patrol Connect servers only on Email or when saving a reusable template.

    Limits: Missouri search warrants on county templates.

  • Narrative Composer

    An AI first-person narrative from case details, transcript, CAD/RMS and officer notes, built from an outline the officer approves.

    Output: Narrative on the clipboard

    Details for Narrative Composer

    Data: Names, SSNs, dates of birth and phone numbers the officer confirms are replaced before processing; plates, VINs and addresses are sent as entered.

    Limits: Unavailable when the agency turns AI features off.

  • Report Analyzer

    AI review of a pasted report: issue cards with severity, why it matters and a suggested revision.

    Output: PDF with feedback

    Details for Report Analyzer

    Data: Confirmed PII is replaced in the browser before anything is sent.

    Limits: Unavailable when the agency turns AI features off.

  • Booking

    The jail's booking form with charge codes autofilled and a medical screening that tells jail staff the action each answer requires.

    Output: PDF emailed to the facility

    Details for Booking

    Data: Keeps no drafts. Typed addresses go to Esri ArcGIS for suggestions.

    Limits: Crawford County Jail is set up today.

  • DWI Investigation Toolkit

    A DWI stop in order, from driving cues through SFSTs, arrest and chemical test, with timers and a timeline.

    Output: PDF summary or narrative text

    Details for DWI Investigation Toolkit

    Data: Drafts saved encrypted on the device.

    Limits: A documentation aid; it never applies decision points.

  • Crash Investigation Toolkit

    An evidence checklist, three calculators and a scene diagram.

    Output: PDF summary with diagram

    Details for Crash Investigation Toolkit

    Data: Drafts saved encrypted on the device.

    Limits: Estimates, not certified reconstruction conclusions.

  • Tow Sheet Generator

    A tow sheet with VIN autofill, tow company presets and a damage diagram.

    Output: Printable PDF

    Details for Tow Sheet Generator

    Data: Address searches go to Esri ArcGIS.

    Limits: Tow company presets are added by an agency administrator.

  • BOLO Generator

    A lookout flyer with caution flags and up to 3 photos.

    Output: PDF flyer or plain text

    Details for BOLO Generator

    Data: Photos are never saved to the draft.

    Limits: Not an NCIC/MULES entry.

  • Miranda Waiver

    The warning in English or Spanish, read aloud, with signatures drawn or typed on screen.

    Output: Signed PDF

    Details for Miranda Waiver

    Data: Drawn signatures are never saved to the device.

    Limits: The printed affirmation is in English for every language.

  • Media Statement Generator

    Seven press release types; copy, download and print are blocked while placeholders remain.

    Output: PDF, clipboard text or text file

    Details for Media Statement Generator

    Data: Draft contents never reach Patrol Connect servers.

    Limits: No Email action.

  • Preservation Letter Generator

    A provider preservation request citing 18 U.S.C. 2703(f)(1).

    Output: Editable text or letterhead PDF

    Details for Preservation Letter Generator

    Data: Drafts saved to the device only.

    Limits: Does not send the letter to the provider.

  • Warrant Service Planner

    A team roster, risk review, communications plan and readiness check.

    Output: PDF

    Details for Warrant Service Planner

    Data: Draft contents reach Patrol Connect servers only on Email.

    Limits: A planning aid; not legal, tactical or command approval.

Utilities

Field and investigative tools

  • VIN Decoder Assistant

    Year, make and model from NHTSA, read aloud.

    Output: On-screen result

    Details for VIN Decoder Assistant

    Data: History encrypted on the device, not synced.

    Limits: 17-character VINs only.

  • Hazmat Quick Reference

    2024 ERG hazards and isolation distances by ID number or material name.

    Output: On-screen guidance

    Details for Hazmat Quick Reference

    Data: The scene checklist stays on the device for up to 14 days.

    Limits: Not a substitute for the printed ERG, the hazmat team or CHEMTREC.

  • Crime Heatmap

    Crime reports entered by agencies on a map, with filters and breakdowns.

    Output: CSV or PDF

    Details for Crime Heatmap

    Data: Map searches go to Esri ArcGIS; map tiles load from CARTO.

    Limits: An investigative aid, not a source of record.

  • IP Lookup Tool

    Location and network details for an IPv4 address, IPv6 address or hostname.

    Output: PDF or CSV

    Details for IP Lookup Tool

    Data: Each uncached lookup is recorded in account-linked audit records.

    Limits: Investigative leads only; not sufficient by itself for probable cause.

  • Security Footage Converter

    AVI security footage to MP4, including damaged-header retail exports.

    Output: MP4 viewing copy

    Details for Security Footage Converter

    Data: Converted on the computer; files are not uploaded.

    Limits: AVI files only; 1 GB per file.

Questions

Do we need to install anything?

No. Officers sign in from a web browser.

Is every module included?

Yes. Agency administrators can turn any module or AI feature off, and a module turned off is blocked on the server, not only hidden.

Does it replace our RMS?

It replaces paper forms, word processor templates, separate reference websites and RMS report-writing add-ons. It does not replace the RMS itself.

Which jails can receive Booking?

Crawford County Jail is set up today. Another facility costs $100 a year, which covers setup and upkeep.

Is officer data used to train AI?

No. Patrol Connect does not let third-party services use agency data for advertising or to train generalized AI models, and does not sell information.

Is Patrol Connect CJIS certified?

BlueShield Solutions LLC does not claim CJIS certification; we design and operate controls to align with CJIS policies and standards relevant to our role as a service provider.

Is it an emergency or dispatch service?

No. Patrol Connect is not an emergency or dispatch service, does not provide legal advice and is not a medical records system.
